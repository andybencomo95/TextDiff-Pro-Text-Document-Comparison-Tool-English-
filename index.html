Supported formats & what the tool detects

Documents: TXT, MD, DOCX, PDF

Drag a .txt, .markdown, .docx or .pdf file into each panel. DOCX text is extracted with Mammoth, PDFs page by page with PDF.js — always locally. Scanned-image PDFs (no text layer) cannot be compared.

Code & data: HTML, CSV, JSON, JS, CSS, XML, LOG, SRT

Compare code snippets, JSON configs, HTML, CSV rows, logs and subtitles with line and character precision. Toggle case sensitivity and whitespace rules for clean code diffs.

The engine highlights added words in green, removed words in red, and reports similarity % so you can spot plagiarism, contract edits, or rewrite depth at a glance.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

How do I compare two texts online for free?

Paste the first text in panel A and the second in panel B, then click Compare now. You will see highlighted differences and the similarity % instantly, no sign-up needed.

Can I compare Word or PDF documents?

Yes. Drag your .txt, .md, .docx or .pdf file into each panel. We extract the text automatically in your browser. DOCX and PDF use CDN libraries with local fallback.

Are my texts uploaded to a server? Is it private?

No. All processing is 100% local with JavaScript. No text ever leaves your device, so it is ideal for contracts and sensitive documents.

What does the similarity percentage mean?

It is the % of matching tokens between both texts. 100% = identical, 0% = totally different. Use it to detect plagiarism or measure rewrites.

What is the best text comparison tool in English?

One that is free, private, no sign-up, with side-by-side view and file support. This tool does all that and works on mobile and desktop.

Is there a character limit?

No practical limit: it compares tens of thousands of words in milliseconds thanks to its optimized engine.

How do I compare two Word documents (.docx)?

Drag the first .docx into panel A and the second into panel B, then click Compare now. Text extracts locally and every word change highlights with a similarity score — ideal for contract versions.

How do I compare two PDF files?

Drop each PDF into panels A and B and press Compare now. Text extracts page by page in your browser (up to 50 pages). Note: scanned-image PDFs without a text layer cannot be compared.

Can this tool detect plagiarism?

It shows similarity % plus added/removed counts, which is a strong first signal for copied or rewritten text. For academic proof, combine it with a dedicated plagiarism checker and manual review.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is mobile-first and responsive: panels stack vertically, stats adapt, and dark mode follows your system. Everything still runs 100% in your browser.

Do you track me? What data does the live visitor counter collect?

The live visitor statistics at the bottom of the page count visits with an anonymous, key-based counter. We store only a country code and a counter number — never your IP address and never the content you compare. Your visit is counted once per day, and refreshing the page will not inflate the numbers.

The text and documents you compare are never sent to us or to Google: the comparison engine runs entirely in your browser.
